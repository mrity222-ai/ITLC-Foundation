About Our Social Welfare Work

As a top social welfare organization in Lucknow, ITLC FOUNDATION is committed to uplifting communities. We focus on education support, women empowerment, and health awareness to build a stronger Uttar Pradesh.

Our Mission

To empower communities in Lucknow through holistic social welfare programs focusing on health, education, and women empowerment. We strive to create a society where everyone has the opportunity to thrive.

Our Vision

We envision a self-reliant Uttar Pradesh where every individual has access to health, education, and a dignified life. Our NGO works to turn this vision into reality.

Our Social Welfare Programs

We run a variety of programs to address the diverse needs of our community in Lucknow and across UP.

Women Empowerment

We run women empowerment programs in Lucknow, providing financial aid and skill development to foster independence.

Health & Sanitation

Our health awareness NGO conducts free health checkups, vaccination drives, and nutrition awareness camps in UP.

Education Support

As an education support NGO in Lucknow, we offer scholarships and literacy programs for children and adults.

Housing & Shelter

Our NGO operates an Old Age Home and provides temporary shelters for the homeless in Lucknow.

Child Welfare Programs

We focus on child welfare through nutrition programs, educational support, and safety workshops.

Elderly & Disabled Welfare

Our social welfare organization provides dedicated pension schemes, care facilities, and support for the elderly.

Jagrukata Abhiyan (Awareness Campaigns)

Spreading knowledge on health, education, and women empowerment is key to long-lasting change in our society.

Health & Hygiene

Campaigns on sanitation, nutrition, and vaccine importance to improve community health in Lucknow.

Women Safety & Empowerment

Conducting workshops and programs in Uttar Pradesh to promote women's safety and empowerment.

Community Workshops

Engaging the community on various social and environmental issues to foster collective growth.
